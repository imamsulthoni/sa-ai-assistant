# Surface Brief: Landing Page (`apps/platform/src/modules/landing/landing-page.tsx`)

## Scope & Visitor Mode
Mode: Persuade
Scope: Complete redesign of the public landing page for Halodocs (AI Systems Analyst Platform).

## Audience, Job, & Action
- Audience: Systems Analysts, Technical Product Managers, Engineering Leads, Architects.
- Job: Rapidly evaluate if Halodocs can replace manual, error-prone BRD drafting and unstructured AI chat tools with a disciplined, audit-grade requirement engine.
- Action: Direct onboarding / registration to open Workspace, explore live interactive workbench simulation, inspect standard FR/BR formats.

## Direction Contract

### THESIS
A high-precision technical instrument for Systems Analysts that rejects generic SaaS marketing tropes and leads with the actual handoff: how an ambiguous user story becomes a decision-ready, engineering-grade specification. The page refuses the standard feature-card wall in favor of one working proof artifact and a clear route into the workspace.

### OWN-WORLD
Quiet graphite studio surfaces (#10110f / #151713), warm high-contrast ink, hairline olive-charcoal rules, and one electric lime signal color (#d9fa54) reserved for action and verified states. Plus Jakarta Sans carries the confident display voice; compact mono labels identify workflow state, source material, and requirement metadata. Surfaces are flat and precise, with depth coming from measured shadows and controlled contrast rather than decorative glass.

### STORY
The visitor first sees the promise in plain language, then toggles a synthetic BRD workbench through ingest, clarification, and approval. The surrounding method section explains why each gate exists: grounded context, focused questions, and explicit diff approval. The path ends with registration into the full workspace, while login remains one click away in the same modal.

### FIRST VIEWPORT
A sticky 72px navigation bar sits over an open graphite field. The left side carries a large two-line statement with “without the guesswork” in lime; the supporting copy and registration action sit beneath it. A restrained index list on the right names traceability, production detail, and human control. The first scroll introduces the workbench as the primary proof, with three tabbed states and responsive stack-down behavior.

### FORM
Technical studio control room, raised by the drum-machine challenger’s single-line state progression and tactile active-state feedback (assigned seed b44334d2; grounded candidate 5).

### FINISH
unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
